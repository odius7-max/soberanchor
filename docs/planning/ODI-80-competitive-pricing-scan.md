# Competitive Pricing Scan — Provider Directory Tiers (ODI-80)

*Claude, 2026-09-17. Web research supporting the provider pricing review. All prices as published or reported at research date; exact source list at bottom. Feeds ODI-80's four questions: Founding Partner framing, Premium gap, side terms, compact-surface disclosure.*

## Executive summary

SoberAnchor's $99/$299 founding ladder sits **well below the addiction-treatment directory market and well above the solo-therapist directory market** — which is the right position, because our sellers are facilities, not individual therapists. The most direct comps (DetoxRehabs.net, Recovery.com) charge $300–$1,000/mo flat and $199–$499/mo respectively for facility listings. Two strategic findings stand out:

1. **Competitors sell verification.** Recovery.com's paid plans are literally named "Verified SUD ($499/mo)" and "Verified MH ($199/mo)"; DetoxRehabs' paid tiers are "Verified" ($300/mo) and "Verified+" ($1,000/mo). SoberAnchor giving the verified badge free with any approved claim is a genuine market differentiator — the marketing currently under-sells it.
2. **The "regularly $199/$499" anchor is our biggest exposure, and no close competitor uses one.** FTC Guides Against Deceptive Pricing (16 CFR §233.1) require a compared-to "former price" to be bona fide — actually and openly offered for a reasonable period. Ours never has been. The vertical's honest players use plain flat prices with clear ad labeling instead.

## What this means for the ODI-80 questions

**1. Founding Partner framing.** Drop the "regularly $199/$499" anchor. Under 16 CFR §233.1 a former-price comparison must reference a price genuinely offered for a reasonable period — $199/$499 never existed. No close competitor anchor-prices this way; DetoxRehabs just states flat prices, Recovery.com just states $499. The honest replacement keeps every benefit of the founding device: **"Early partner rate — locked for your first 12 months. Pricing for new subscribers rises as the directory grows."** True, defined, urgent. If we want a boundary, define it concretely (first 100 facilities, or through a stated date) — undefined "founding" status reads as fake scarcity.

**2. Premium gap ($99 → $299, 3×).** The vertical says the multiplier is normal: DetoxRehabs runs $300 → $1,000 (3.3×), Recovery.com $199 → $499 (2.5×). The gap is not the problem — the *contents* are. Post-ODI-79 (fabricated analytics removed), Premium = labeled Featured placement + call-tracking (unbuilt) + quarterly report + priority support. Competitors justify their top tiers with campaign managers, copywriters, retargeting (Recovery.com Premium). Recommendation: keep $299, and make the quarterly performance report *real and early* (even a manually assembled one) — it's the cheapest credible Premium anchor while call-tracking gets built.

**3. Price levels.** We are cheap for the vertical, deliberately. Founding $99 is a third of DetoxRehabs' entry paid tier; regular $199 would still undercut it. Given facilities already pay $3,095/yr just for LegitScript renewal and $2,500–8,000 per admission on aggregator leads, a $1,188/yr Enhanced subscription is a rounding error *if the directory sends any families at all* — confirming the real constraint is traffic proof, not price. Land-grab pricing now, raise with published traffic numbers later, is the right sequence. No change recommended.

**4. Side terms.** Annual 2-months-free is standard (GoodTherapy ~10%; ours ~17% — fine). The outpatient 50% discount has no market precedent found; it's defensible mission-wise but adds a second price for every tier. Suggest keeping it as an unadvertised accommodation ("outpatient-only program? contact us") rather than a headline term, until billing exists.

**5. Compact-surface disclosure (already in flight on the branch).** Wherever a price shows, carry the qualifier ("$99/mo Founding rate"). Competitors' failure mode is the opposite one — hidden pricing behind sales calls (AlcoholHelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc) — and our published-flat-price posture is worth keeping loudly.

**Marketing angle unlocked by this research:** competitors charge $300–$500/mo *for the word "Verified."* We should say, on /for-providers, some version of: "Verification is free here. It's earned by claiming your listing, not bought." That is a true, sharp differentiator and it reinforces the trust positioning with families too.

## Regulatory footnotes

- **16 CFR Part 233** (Guides Against Deceptive Pricing): §233.1 former-price comparisons must be bona fide; advertising a made-up "regular" price to show a discount is the textbook violation.
- **EKRA (18 U.S.C. §220)** and state brokering laws (FL §817.505, CA SB 1228): why per-lead/per-admission is off the table; industry guidance now steers even aggregators toward fixed-fee structures not contingent on admissions. Our flat-only spec rule is validated by the market's own drift.
- **FL FDACS marketing-service licensure**: AlcoholHelp holds license #TS62 — worth a check (separate issue) whether SoberAnchor's model requires one as it scales into paid placement in Florida.
- **LegitScript** is the de-facto gate for paid *advertising* by facilities; ODI-54's licensure-verification gate aligns with what Recovery.com already requires at onboarding.
